4. Higher education — 2014 starting line

Higher-education institutions

Higher-ed enrolment

The official 2014-15 baseline is unusually strong here: 51,534 HEIs, 760 universities, 38,498 colleges and 3.42 crore higher-education enrolment. By June 2025, the government reports 70,018 HEIs, 1,338 universities and 52,081 colleges; higher-ed enrolment reached 4.46 crore in 2022-23. citeturn0search0turn0search12turn0search11

This dashboard is intended for evidence-based political/public-policy comparison. Education is a Concurrent List subject, so Union and State governments both influence outcomes. A change in a national indicator is not automatically attributable to the Union government alone.
