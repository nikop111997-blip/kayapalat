It separates startup ecosystem growth, MSME formalisation, credit, entrepreneurship, jobs, exports, manufacturing and business climate — including negative and unresolved indicators.

7. Exports & industrial business — add these as a separate macro layer

For a politically serious dashboard, exports should not be mixed with startup funding. Add annual merchandise exports, services exports, total exports, manufacturing GVA, FDI inflows, corporate investment and GST collections from RBI/DGFT/Commerce/PIB. These have different definitions and should be shown as annual time series rather than a single “NDA achieved” number.
Recommended series
Exports
Merchandise, services and total exports — 2013-14 → latest.
Recommended series
FDI
Annual FDI inflow and equity inflow — same definition across years.
Recommended series
GST
Gross GST collections, taxpayers and compliance — post-2017 series.
Recommended series
Manufacturing
Manufacturing GVA, employment and factory output.

Method: 2014 is the baseline. The dashboard does not pretend Startup India existed in 2014 — it explicitly marks 2014 as “not launched” and measures the post-2014 ecosystem that was created. For business indicators where a genuine same-definition 2014 series is unavailable, it says so instead of manufacturing a comparison.

Use this as an evidence dashboard, not as a claim that every movement was caused solely by the Union government. State policy, global capital cycles, COVID-19, RBI policy, technology shifts and private investment also affect these indicators.
